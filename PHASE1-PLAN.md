## 7. CRITIQUE — the single riskiest assumption + what could still break the whole overlay

**Riskiest assumption:** that PrairieLearn's **server-set parent-page CSP admits a same-origin `<link>` to `clientFilesCourse/chrome.css`**. The de-risk is strong but circumstantial — the iframe already loads `style.css`/wasm from `clientFilesCourse` under `'self'` (proving same origin), and PL already executes an inline `<script>` in the parent (`pl-scribble.py`) — yet the coded mechanism (`<link>`) is **not** the one the earlier probe validated (inline `<style>`), the directive is different (`style-src` source-list vs inline), and **CSP stripping of a `<link>` is completely silent**: no console error, no exception, just an **unstyled rail** in front of a live exam. A **nonce-based** `style-src` on the UIUC instance would strip **both** the `<link>` and the fallback `<style>`. This is why the flip's gate is a `getComputedStyle` assert on a **chrome.css-only** property on the **actual UIUC hosted instance** — and why the merged `<link>`→inline-`<style>` fallback (B1-2) exists. If this assumption is wrong and undetected, the whole overlay ships broken.

**The other two things that could break the whole overlay:** (1) **The calc-dodge realm translation (B3-8 part 2) is the ONLY calculator mitigation for the reparented rail** — the parent-realm rail escapes the iframe clip entirely, so a sign error or a stale `frameElement` rect parks the rail **into** the calculator hole and re-opens click-eating during the exam; there is no clip to fall back on. (2) **Item-12 geometry (Decision 1) shipped as the silent full-width default (Option A)** puts a full-browser-viewport bar across PL's own header on a live CBTF exam — functionally fine, but it reads as "the tool broke," which in a high-stakes pilot is indistinguishable from an actual break. All three are gated: Decision 1 and Decision 2 are user sign-offs, and the CSP + calc mitigations are NO-GO rows on the matrix — **none of the code lands as a flip until every box in §4/§5 is checked, and the gate at `app.js:116` stays `false` (provably inert) until then.**
